# The Day in World News — 20 November 2026

A program that draws one image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026. Lead story: Cyclone Akvan makes landfall on India's east coast. Also inside: a tanker fire after a mine strike in the Strait of Hormuz, Russian strikes damaging the Austrian and Dutch embassies in Kyiv, South Korea's exports passing $1 trillion for the first time, the day after the Shenzhen trilateral and its nuclear pledge, and Malaysia's PM flagging air force officers in a drug probe.

Run `python3 main.py` — it renders `news.png` (1024×1024) and copies it to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.
